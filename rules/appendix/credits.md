# Credits and attribution

This ruleset is original text. No third-party rules text is reproduced verbatim, so no licence notice is
required (14.8.1, DL-14-11). The designs below informed it. Each rule that draws on one cites it in place
with `[R: …]` or a research-digest reference (`_process/research/R1`–`R7`).

**Games and rules designs consulted:** *Dungeons & Dragons* (5e SRD) and *Old-School Essentials*
(retainers, morale, encounter procedures); *Pathfinder 2e* (three-action economy, dying); *Ironsworn* and
*Starforged* (oracles, truths, solo procedures); *Mythic GME* (oracle odds); *Blades in the Dark* (clocks,
position and effect); *Burning Wheel* (advancement by use); *Cairn* and *Mausritter* (lean harm,
inventory); *The One Ring* (journeys); *13th Age* (backgrounds); *Worlds Without
Number* (factions, faction turns); *Darkest Dungeon* (afflictions, stress); *Baldur's Gate 3* (reactive
play).

**Fiction that shaped the design goals:** Brandon Sanderson's Cosmere (the *Ars Arcanum* model for the
codex, constitution P7; *Mistborn*'s rule-bound magic); LitRPG traditions such as *He Who Fights With
Monsters* (recognition and tracked complexity).

**Safety tools:** lines and veils (Ron Edwards, popularised by the TTRPG Safety Toolkit), the X-card (John
Stavropoulos), and the adjustment tools catalogued in R5 (P-10), adapted for one player and an AI DM (12.5.6,
12.5.7).

Exact sources, with URLs, are listed in `_process/reports/verification-citations.md` and in the research
digests.
