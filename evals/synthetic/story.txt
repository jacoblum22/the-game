The story so far.

The city of Mourhold, in the empire of Vaenyr, is divided between two kinds of power. Sartha, the Old Goddess of Shadows, Sacrifice and Survival, works through shadow and subversion. Lir, the god of order, works through the Bleak Wards and the Covenant of Lir. Shaedryn "Shae" Velaris is Sartha's champion and bears the Shard of Sartha. Sartha has told her that the gods lie. There is a whispered myth called the Gods' Stone, the origin of the divine fracture, which Eira the Silent has only found fragments of. Nobody has taken it up.

Shae leads the Ascendant Circle, a reincarnating fellowship of divine champions that meets in the Veiled Vigil, beneath the Shade's Refuge. Elara Velaris, her distant cousin and betrothed, runs the Refuge. Rhylla Greysong is her spymaster and oldest companion. Galen Orinel, called Ash, is a quiet Vyrkani enforcer. Eira the Silent is an exiled priestess who prays at the altar of Elyndra. Koryth Mal'Nereth is the youngest, a paladin who bears the Shard of Aurenis, with Calyth Neran, his ranger companion. Maera Liss is a close ally, but Elara and Rhylla distrust her. Shae remembers their past lives first.

House Velaris was ruled by Shae's mother, Maelis Velaris, who exiled her and allied with the Bleak Wards. Shae returned, purged the traitors, and confronted Maelis at the Veiled Manor. There Maelis swore: "Then let the shadows take me too. For House Velaris." Shae answered: "For us." A tearful reconciliation followed. Maelis's loyalty is sworn but proud. Therrin Velaris was executed and Darius Velaris is dead. Evander Velaris, the High Court diplomat, supports Shae and watches closely. Tayla and Jared Velaris are her allies in the family. Elder Thamiel Velaris, the Warden of Secrets, advises the Archon and is guarded but no longer openly opposed. Winning him over or removing him is the open quest.

The Shadowcloaks are the former Graycloaks, reshaped under Lirian, with Soraya Windstride and Jarek, after the Circle killed the paranoid Master Whisper. Their base is the Shade's Vigil, beside the Refuge.

The merchants are Shae's wider net. Celeste, a trader whose caravans the Wards block at the eastern docks, is a close ally. Bellum Avarelle, a cautious widow, is bound to Shae by the oath in the Binding Parlor. Their bond is close but not romantic. Veyra Nox is a smuggler and former member of the Pale, an informal ally who is not Syndicate. The Sable Syndicate is led by Silas Crowley. Opposed to Shae are Guildmaster Tolan Vehris, and Liraine Vos, a priestess of Valencia. Her cousin Halran Vos holds the swing vote on dock access. He launders money for the Wards, and his daughter Ilaen is tied to a scandal. Lord Hareth Dorne holds the rotating seat. Silva Dray, head of the Arcane Bazaar, is unmet. House Durn, with Ranna Durn, and House Rathven, with Mira Rathven, are still undeclared or opposed.

The enemies are the Bleak Wards under Archon Varyn Morthal, with Aric Ironshade, Seraphine Lockeward and Alandar Redhammer. Duke Elias Corvan leads the Covenant of Lir, which calls Shae a false prophet.

Open threads: the rumour that the Wards are developing anti-shard rituals; the Gods' Stone; Veyra's rumoured dealings with a Pale remnant; Gregor, who wants to use the Circle; and the Silent Veil's Eclipse Vigil. The open quests are to consolidate House Velaris and to open the eastern docks for Celeste.

Tomorrow at highsun the Merchant Conclave votes on the Eastern Docks Access Act in the Merchant Guildhall. Vehris is against it, Celeste and Bellum are for it, and Halran Vos and Hareth Dorne are uncertain. Shae has written to Bellum and Celeste. She wears House Velaris battle regalia, and Rhylla, in a garnet gown, will escort her. The Shadowcloaks are inside the hall and outside it. Shae and Rhylla are about to enter.
