SUMMARY:
1. Hale and Garrow's movements and knowledge do not add up: Hale warns Osric about Wren before anyone could have told him; Garrow is at Thornhallow's kitchens a day before he followed Wren, and he came 'upriver' to a place downriver. The villain's information network is the weakest part of the Thornhallow plot.
2. The Lantern's Rest lamp is explained three different ways (Corvin gave it, Corvin first marvelled at it, Elowen left it twenty years ago). This is the main clue to Wren's origin, so the inconsistency matters. Related: the Dray forgery is described as already sold and in effect in R25, then as a contract still to be sealed in R48.
3. Travel and timing slip when the story needs it: the wagon's head start becomes 'an hour or two' after being established as seven hours; the three-day Thornhallow-to-Saltmarsh road is covered in about 34 hours while exhausted; dusk shifts by an hour between days. Corvin's escape from a crowded ford is unexplained. Also note that rounds 54-60 contain no narration (only API error text), so the Vantry hall scene is never delivered and the story ends mid-scene.
