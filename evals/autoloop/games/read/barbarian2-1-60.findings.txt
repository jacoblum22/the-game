3. The DM honors bad rolls and gives them consequences: the lost goat-path (twice), the failed ambush that leads to capture, the failed stealth at the culvert and the Counting House. Natural 20s pay off (the cairn clue, the Insight on Gorrak's fear, the Intimidation on the scouts).
