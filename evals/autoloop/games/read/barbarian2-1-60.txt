The mountains stand over the lowlands like a wall, and everything beneath them lives with an eye turned upward. Herders drive their flocks down from the high pastures to the market towns, and some come down with stories of the warbands that raid the passes. The Iron Tallies are the worst of those stories: hobgoblins who count their raids in notches.

Korvash Emberhide is a red-scaled, broad-shouldered dragonborn, loud and proud, and he is the son of a clan of mountain herders. When he was a boy, the Iron Tallies overran his village. His father Dargan held the Goat's Throat pass, losing a hand and then his life, so the herd and the children could flee. His sister Tessa was lost in the raid, and no one ever learned her fate. Korvash was raised in Harrowmere by his Aunt Veshra. He carries his father's second-best greataxe, with Dargan's ember-red scale-sash tied to the haft, and he has spent years making sure no one weak is bullied while he stands nearby.

Dusk settles over Harrowmere's market square. Lanterns are being lit and stallholders are folding awnings. Three young toughs scatter into the alleys, one of them clutching a wrist and swearing, and they leave behind an overturned cart of cheeses and a family of herders huddled against it. The mother is gathering the wheels back into her apron, and the father is nursing a split lip.

A thin boy of about twelve, mud to the knees, breaks away from them and runs up to you, breathless. "You're Korvash, aren't you? Veshra's nephew?
