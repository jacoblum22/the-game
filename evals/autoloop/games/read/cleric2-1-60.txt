Brannoc Ashdown is a young cleric of the Life domain from Millbrook Landing, a river town of fishermen and reed-cutters. The Grey Fever emptied the houses there when he was six, and the old temple healer, Mother Edda Thorne, raised him. She died last winter and left him her plain wooden amulet, and he left town to carry her work to places that have no healer. He is only a few weeks into that road.

Dusk is settling over a muddy back road in a country of thin farms and low hills. A small settlement huddles ahead, a scatter of crooked houses with its lanterns lit early and its doors barred. A dog barks once and stops. The air smells of wet earth, woodsmoke, and, faintly under it, blood.

A boy of about ten, mud to his knees, comes running out from between two fences and nearly slips as he catches your sleeve. "Please, are you a healer? That's a healer's amulet, isn't it? Please, sir, they're in the Harrow barn. My da, and Widow Harrow's boys, and... there's so many. Something came out of the woods last night. We've no one to help them. Please."

Behind him, past the last house, a big barn stands with its doors shut and a thin line of lamplight leaking from the cracks. Somewhere inside, someone is moaning. At the treeline beyond, nothing moves.
