SUMMARY:
1. Tessa's whereabouts do not add up. She is a recluse in the ledger-house who cannot even climb to the cairn, yet she leaves cheese at Harrowmere's inn, notes at the cairn on Day 10-11, and later visits the Throat on a dozen nights alone. Her account of the night of the raid also changes from hiding alone on the ledge to dragging Korvash there.
2. Distances and travel times are unstable: Greyshaft is three days from the Throat (R25-R29, R63), then a night's march (R64), then a day and a half to Harrowmere for children (R101); Harrowmere to Cinder Ford is a night's march (R1, R24) and then a day and a half (R116). Gorrak reaches the road within hours of a rider being sent.
3. Counts and loyalties drift in the Greyshaft climax: the Tin Row's ninety-four all escape, yet dozens more children and sixty miners appear; the Tally-sworn stair guards are Gorrak's men and then the Foreman's; the Foreman's rider is at the Stones, then not, then forgotten; Gorrak's forty spears appear from nowhere; and the cairn's origin is told three incompatible ways.
