3. The Long Cut sequence has careless continuity in its details: the fled ghoul's wounds and max HP reset, the number of bodies shifts between four and five, Dorrin's injury changes to an arm, the gap's position moves, and Widow Harrow's praise misstates how long Brannoc stayed (R29-R35).
