SUMMARY:
1. Travel times and distances are unstable between Harrowmere, the Stones, the Throat, Cinder Ford and Greyshaft. Korvash rides to the Throat in 5.5 hours, though Cinder Ford below it is a day and a half out. Marn's wall falls the same night as a raid 1.5 days away. Greyshaft is three days from the cairn, then about a day and a quarter from the Stones. A trip from the cut to town to the Stones fits in two hours. These keep reappearing in the second half and undercut the pursuit and ambush plots.
2. Grunna's Tolk arc does not follow its own setup. The die says Tolk holds to his deadline, yet he arrives at the earliest possible time. Skarn is first the senior survivor and then Tolk's underling. The Weeping Ledge changes from a small secret hollow to a wide shelf that enemy scouts also know. A third, 'second' ledger appears after the story closed the ledger plot with fire.
3. Threads and facts are dropped or left unanswered, especially around the failures. Tessa's and Ushk's testimony at the hearing, Pell's request, and the scouts' fate are never resolved. The burial is stated as done and then as upcoming. Brakk's reply to the question the player actually asked, at R177, is never given to the player.
